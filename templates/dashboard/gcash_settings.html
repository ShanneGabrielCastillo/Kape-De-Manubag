ℹ️ How Manual GCash Verification Works

  1. Customer selects GCash at checkout and places their order.
  2. Customer sees your GCash number/QR and the exact amount to pay.
  3. Customer pays via their GCash app.
  4. Customer enters the GCash reference number and optionally uploads a screenshot.
  5. Staff receives a notification and sees the submission in Order Management.
  6. Staff checks the actual GCash account to confirm the payment.
  7. Staff clicks Verify Payment — order moves to Preparing.
  8. Customer is notified in real-time and sees Order Received.

⚠️ Never click Verify without checking your actual GCash account first. The customer's reference number alone is not proof of payment.
